How the simpler rail works

This single graph explains the simplified design: cards are small because they no longer carry support beats or section indexes. Their vertical position identifies the matching Markdown section, while the summary appears after the note instead of in the left rail.

Placement

The hook cards live in the editor's left cue margin, aligned beside note content rather than centered as a separate mode surface.

Density

Each note section gets a compact card sized for vertical alignment. The card should not include section indexes, support beats, or loud status words.

Interaction

Clicking a card scrolls or focuses the corresponding Markdown section. No study, reveal, or quiz behavior is part of this design.
