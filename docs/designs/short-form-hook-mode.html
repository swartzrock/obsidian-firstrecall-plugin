Design posture

Energy comes from scale, color, and pacing. Semantics stay plain: question, beats, and summary are real text so themes, selection, and accessibility still work.

1. Entry point

Study Mode toolbar: add a temporary Hook Sprint toggle near existing display controls. Keep the default calm Cornell surface unchanged.

2. Card anatomy

Hook title, staged beats, answer action: each card maps to one usable cue row and carries the original question for fidelity checks.

3. Failure states

No silent gaps: missing or failed cues render as compact skipped cards with regenerate copy so the sprint does not lose orientation.
