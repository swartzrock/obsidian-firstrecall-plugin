What each rail label means

This single graph turns the Hook Mode vocabulary into implementation rules: section numbers map cards to Markdown sections, status labels describe reveal/focus state, support beats are the tappable hints, and the final card is the summary synthesis after all section cards.

Placement

The hook cards live in the editor's left cue margin, aligned beside note content rather than centered as a separate mobile sprint.

Density

Each note section gets a compact card. The active section card can expand slightly; queued cards stay glanceable.

Interaction

Beats reveal in place, while clicking a card scrolls or focuses the corresponding Markdown section.
