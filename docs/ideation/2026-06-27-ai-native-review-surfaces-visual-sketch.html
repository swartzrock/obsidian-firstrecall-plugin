CueCraft visual probe

AI-native review surfaces

The old Cornell model assumes the learner writes cues by hand. These sketches ask what CueCraft should do when AI can identify salience, generate summaries, stage recall, and keep a memory loop running directly inside a normal Obsidian note.

Judge behavior, not styling. Pick the direction that most improves recall while staying useful during normal reading. Ignore exact colors, typography, and spacing.

1. Signal Box: AI highlights what the reader should notice

The side box stops being a question card and becomes a section-level salience layer: key claims, terms, contrasts, and phrases worth remembering. The note remains the center; CueCraft tells the reader where to put attention.

Best first bet
Claude for Execs

Transform Product Dev

  1. Transformative orgs combine frontier AI models with proprietary data and deep domain expertise.
  2. The trust boundary determines what can ship when client data, audit trails, and model access are regulated.
  3. Success includes reduced cycle time, higher quality, and fewer critical errors.
  4. Managed agents lower coordination cost and create durable operational infrastructure.

Claude for the rest of the org

  1. Cowork works without a custom build, using plugins for role-specific expertise.
  2. Admins configure marketplaces so teams avoid unsanctioned tools.

Signals in this section

  • Trust boundary controls what AI-enabled workflows can ship
  • Proprietary data is the moat, not the model alone
  • Reduced cycle time is the measurable outcome

2. Section Lens: one-sentence memory card per section

Each section gets a compact explanation in the style of the example: one sentence, bolded critical phrase, and a plain-English why. This is less quiz-like and more like an AI study companion translating notes into retainable meaning.

Best for reading comprehension
Landscape Research Report

Terms

Focus on agent autonomy: an agent differs from a chatbot because it can plan, use tools, and complete work across multiple steps.
  1. Agent - Independent AI that can plan, decide, and use tools to complete a task.
  2. Chatbot - single-turn interaction AI app. Not good at multi-step tasks.
  3. Claude - foundation for chatbot and agents, extended with MCP, skills, plugins.
  4. Plugins - customize Claude for a role/team, with tools and workflows.

How To Upskill Employees

Remember encoded expertise: organizations get faster when standards and workflows are turned into reusable plugins or agents.
  1. Tailor AI on your standards, terms, tools, and institutional knowledge.
  2. Teach Claude methodology, compliance, brand voice, and reporting standards.

Section takeaway

The important shift is from conversation to delegated work.

Good for readers who do not want to quiz yet but need the meaning compressed.

Ask yourself

Could you explain why tool use changes the category from chatbot to agent?

3. Recall Ladder: staged reveal instead of passive questions

The rail becomes a small practice loop: gist first, then cloze, then question, then answer check. It encourages active recall without turning the whole note into a separate study mode.

Best for retention practice
cloddy
Step 1 - gist
Agents do work across steps.
Step 2 - fill blank
An agent differs by using ____ and ____.
Step 3 - recall
What makes an agent more capable than a chatbot?
Type or think the answer before reveal

Terms

  1. Agent - Independent AI that can plan, decide, and use tools to complete a task.
  2. Chatbot - single-turn interaction AI app. Not good at multi-step tasks.
  3. MCP - enables Claude to CRUD with external services.
  4. Skills - predefined workflows for specific tasks.

Who It Is For

  • Students reviewing lecture notes, readings, book notes, and outlines.
  • Researchers turning dense notes into recall prompts.

4. Note Brief: Otter-style overview plus section cards

Instead of attaching one prompt to each heading, CueCraft creates a note-level brief at the top: what this note is about, what matters, open questions, and recommended review order. Section cards below it stay aligned with the original notes.

CueCraft tracks which sections deserve attention and why: weak recall, stale content, high importance, or repeated mistakes. The left side stops mirroring headings and becomes a review queue for the whole note.

Decision frame

These are not mutually exclusive forever, but the first product bet should choose one primary learning loop. The safest direction is Signal Box plus Section Lens: immediate value while reading, low interaction burden, and a stronger foundation for later recall practice.
